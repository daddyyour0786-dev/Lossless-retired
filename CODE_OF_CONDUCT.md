# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The LSY Free community aims to be welcoming, respectful, and constructive. We want people with different backgrounds and levels of experience to be able to participate in project discussions and contribute safely.

## Expected behavior

Community members, contributors, and maintainers are expected to:

- Be respectful, considerate, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and technical decisions without attacking people.
- Accept good-faith criticism and differing viewpoints.
- Give clear, actionable feedback.
- Respect other people's privacy and obtain permission before sharing private information.
- Keep discussions relevant and help make participation accessible to newcomers.

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Insults, deliberate intimidation, trolling, or sustained disruptive behavior.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or sexualized language.
- Publishing private information, credentials, or private communications without permission.
- Spam, malicious links, or deliberately derailing project discussions.
- Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This code applies to project spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, code reviews, and other spaces where someone is representing or participating in the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the repository owner, [@daddyyour0786-dev](https://github.com/daddyyour0786-dev), through their GitHub profile. Please avoid posting sensitive details publicly.

Reports will be reviewed as fairly and discreetly as reasonably possible. The maintainer may ask for relevant context and will respect the privacy of everyone involved to the extent practical.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues, commits, discussions, or pull requests that violate this code. Depending on the severity and frequency of the behavior, the maintainer may issue a warning or restrict participation in project spaces.

Retaliation for making a good-faith report will not be tolerated.
